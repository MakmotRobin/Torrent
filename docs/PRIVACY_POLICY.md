# Privacy Policy for PulseTorrent

*Last updated: November 2026*

PulseTorrent is an open, decentralized peer-to-peer (P2P) BitTorrent client for Windows. Your privacy is paramount, and this application is built with a **Zero-Telemetry, Privacy-First Architecture**.

---

### 1. Zero Telemetry & Analytics
* PulseTorrent does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).
* We do not track your IP address, your search queries, downloaded file names, or application usage statistics.
* There are no embedded third-party tracking SDKs, advertising trackers, or user behavioral analytics.

---

### 2. Local Data Storage
All application configurations and states are stored **100% locally on your computer**:
* **Settings & Preferences:** Saved in `%USERPROFILE%\.torrent-desktop-app\settings.json`.
* **Active Torrent State:** Resume data and file download paths are saved in `%USERPROFILE%\.torrent-desktop-app\torrents.json`.
* **Downloaded Files:** Stored only in the destination directory chosen by the user.

No application data is ever synced to developer-operated cloud servers.

---

### 3. Peer-to-Peer Network Communication
* As part of the standard decentralized BitTorrent protocol specification, your device's external IP address and listening port are shared with other connected swarm peers, trackers, and the Distributed Hash Table (DHT) network exclusively to facilitate file piece discovery and data transfer.
* PulseTorrent does not monitor, inspect, or log peer network traffic.

---

### 4. Payments and Microsoft Store In-App Purchases
* All purchases (such as upgrading to **PulseTorrent Pro**) are processed directly and securely by the **Microsoft Store Commerce Engine**.
* We do not collect, process, or have access to your credit card, bank, or billing address information. All payment processing is governed by [Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/).

---

### 5. Contact & Support
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or application compliance, please reach out via GitHub Issues or contact developer support.
